# Research Papers — Usman Kalera

Research papers by **Usman Kalera** (BS Artificial Intelligence, Superior University, Lahore) —
spanning speech AI, video advertising, digital identity, and face-association research.
All papers are open access (CC BY 4.0) with DOIs on Zenodo & Figshare.

## Papers

### 1. Switch-Point Prosody: Pitch Variability as a Marker of Code-Switch Boundaries in TTS
Code-switched (Urdu–English) text-to-speech prosody — F0 measurements at switch points
(51.9 vs 29.8 Hz pitch variability), a TTS demo, and a reproducible 3-phase experimental protocol.
- DOI (Zenodo): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23368075
- DOI (Figshare): https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.34500021
- PDF: [`switch-point-prosody.pdf`](switch-point-prosody.pdf)

### 2. Do AI-Generated Video Advertisements Work for Pakistan's Small Businesses?
A research proposal with an honest experimental design for testing AI-generated video ads
with small businesses in Pakistan.
- DOI (Zenodo): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23368307
- DOI (Figshare): https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.34500144
- PDF: [`ai-video-ads-pakistan.pdf`](ai-video-ads-pakistan.pdf)

### 3. LinkedIn Identity Verification and Pakistan's CNIC Holders
Why LinkedIn's identity verification fails for Pakistani CNIC holders — and what it means
for professional access.
- DOI (Zenodo): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23372052
- DOI (Figshare): https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.34503759
- PDF: [`linkedin-cnic-verification-pakistan.pdf`](linkedin-cnic-verification-pakistan.pdf)

### 4. Node-Level Identity Attention for Multi-Face Frames: A Controlled Landmark-Graph Study
Phase 2 — a synthetic controlled study (MediaPipe 468-landmark pipeline, fixed seed 20261014,
200 trials/condition, 4 ablations): association accuracy 0.620 → 1.000, wrong-ID margin
−0.029 → −0.829. Honest limitations and a Phase 3 protocol included.
- DOI (Zenodo): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23376168
- DOI (Figshare): https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.34515273
- PDF: [`node-level-identity-attention-phase2.pdf`](node-level-identity-attention-phase2.pdf)
